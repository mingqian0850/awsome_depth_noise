- **摘要**: Foothold-constrained terrain is characterized by sparse, discontinuous, or geometrically restricted feasible foot contacts, as encountered on stepping stones, across gaps, and on narrow stair treads. On such terrain, a single misstep often leaves little room to recover, so policies that base foot-placement decisions primarily on the immediately visible terrain are prone to failure. We ask whether a learned predictive summary of near-future observations and rewards can provide the anticipatory information required in such settings.
